⚠️ THE HARD TRUTH ABOUT OPTIONS BUYING

SEBI data shows 89% of retail options traders lose money. Options have theta decay — your option loses value every day even if Nifty doesn't move. You are competing with algo traders and FIIs who have better data and faster execution.

Your app gives you a real edge. But the edge only works with discipline.

💡 WHY YOUR APP ALONE IS NOT ENOUGH

Most losses come from position sizing, ignoring stop-losses, and overtrading — not bad signals. Your job is to fix those three things first.

1 PAPER TRADE FOR 30 DAYS FIRST
📓 WHAT IS PAPER TRADING?

When your app shows BUY CALL or BUY PUT, write it in a notebook — as if you bought. No real money. Then check what happened. This verifies whether YOUR signal works on YOUR market conditions before risking capital.

  • 1
    Open app at 9:50 AM dailyADX and MTF need 35 min to warm up. Signals before this are unreliable.
  • 2
    Wait for signal with confidence ≥65%All gates must be green. Ignore any WAIT signal completely.
  • 3
    Note Nifty price + ATM premiumCheck NSE option chain for the CE or PE premium at that moment.
  • 4
    Set paper SL and targetSL = VIX-dynamic (see AI card). At VIX 14 (25% SL): ₹80 premium → SL ₹60, target ₹120 (1:2 R:R). Adjust SL% with VIX level.
  • 5
    Check every 15 min, record outcomeDid it hit target? Hit SL? Write ALL outcomes — wins AND losses.
  • 6
    Do 30 trading days (~6 weeks) before real moneyYou need 20+ signal samples to know if the system has positive expectancy.
⚠️

If paper trades show more losses than wins after 30 days — do NOT start real trading. Review which gates were failing. Tune the signal, not add money pressure.

2 THE ONLY 6 RULES THAT MATTER
🖨️ PRINT THIS. PUT IT NEXT TO YOUR SCREEN.
  • RULE 1 — Never trade before 10:00 AM. The first 45 min are for gap-filling. Your app blocks this. Honor it.
  • RULE 2 — Never trade if WAIT is showing. If one gate fails, there is no excuse to enter. WAIT means wait.
  • RULE 3 — Set your SL before you enter. Never remove it once placed. Never move it wider.
  • RULE 4 — Maximum 1 trade per day. One focused trade beats 5 impulsive ones.
  • RULE 5 — Risk only ₹2,000–3,000 per trade until you have 3 months of consistent profit.
  • RULE 6 — Close everything by 14:30. No exceptions. Theta in the last hour will eat your premium.
"The goal of a beginner is not to make money. The goal is to not lose money while you learn. Profit comes later, after discipline is built."
4 PRE-TRADE CHECKLIST
✅ GO THROUGH THIS BEFORE EVERY ENTRY
  • Time is between 10:00 AM and 14:00 PM?
  • Signal is BUY CALL or BUY PUT (not WAIT)?
  • All quality gates are green (MTF, ADX ≥20, RSI, VIX <20, S/R clear)?
  • Confidence is ≥65%?

  • Do NOT enter on Monday before 10:30 or Friday (expiry day) after 13:00.
  • Do NOT enter if major news expected in next 1 hour (RBI, US Fed, election). App cannot predict news shocks.
5 POSITION SIZING & STOP-LOSS
🚨 #1 CAUSE OF BLOWING ACCOUNTS

Beginners lose everything not from bad signals — but from buying too many lots when wrong. One bad trade wiped 10 winning trades. This is called over-sizing.

📊 SAFE SIZING BY ACCOUNT SIZE
ACCOUNTMAX RISK/TRADELOTS
₹25,000₹1,5001 lot
₹50,000₹2,5001 lot
₹1,00,000₹4,0001–2 lots
₹2,00,000+₹6,000–8,0002 lots max
📐 STOP-LOSS RULES
  • SL = VIX-dynamic: 20% (VIX<12) · 25% (VIX 12-16) · 30% (VIX 16-20) · 35% (VIX>20). Example at VIX 14: bought CE @ ₹100 → SL @ ₹75. Exit immediately. Target = 2× SL risk (₹150) for 1:2 R:R.
  • Never "average down" (buy more when price falls). This is how accounts blow up.
  • Target = 50–60% gain. Example: bought CE @ ₹100 → target @ ₹155.
  • If trade reaches +30% profit → move SL to break-even. Lock in safety.
  • Nifty lot size = 50. So 1 lot × ₹1 move in premium = ₹50 profit or loss.
💡

Winning 40% of trades can still be profitable IF winners are 2× losers. Win ₹3,000 × 4 trades, lose ₹1,500 × 6 trades = +₹12,000 − ₹9,000 = +₹3,000 net.
